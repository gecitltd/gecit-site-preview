**Antonini Srl** manufactures the lehrs used for annealing and tempering container and tableware glass and for decorating finished glass products. Thanks to their structural technology and superior technical characteristics, Antonini lehrs have reached a significant position in the glass sector. Around 2000 Antonini lehrs are in operation worldwide.

Antonini lehrs offer:

- flexibility, allowing container and tableware glass from either automatic or manual production to be annealed,
- a special system that makes the lehr quick to install,
- low energy consumption, achieved through careful attention to every detail and continuous updating of the systems,
- excellent thermal insulation, as every section of the lehr is built by hand,
- high-quality material selection,
- a homogeneous and stable temperature distribution inside the lehr, thanks to the air circulation system,
- automatic and precise adjustment and control of lehr temperature through modern equipment,
- easy and quick maintenance,
- a heating system that can run on gas, oil or electricity,
- qualified and responsive technical service.
