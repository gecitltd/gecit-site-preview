**Iris Inspection Machines** produces inline inspection systems for the container glass industry. As a leader in technology and innovation, the company anticipates customer needs and develops long-term, low-cost products for container glass quality control. With a strong commitment to research and development, the IRIS team works continuously on advanced versions of the Evolution machines, improving inspection accuracy and enabling defect detection so that productivity in container glass production is maximised.

## Evolution 12 – 16

A machine for inspecting sidewall defects and sidewall stress. Available with either 12 or 16 cameras.

![Evolution 12-16](/img/iris/image001.png)

## Evolution 5

A machine for inspecting bottle base and finish defects and base stress, available with a mould number reader system.

![Evolution 5](/img/iris/image003.png)

## Evolution 1

A stand-alone machine for finish defect inspection.

![Evolution 1](/img/iris/image005.png)

## Evolution ultimate

A machine for inspecting transparent and low-contrast defects in perfume bottles and cosmetics packaging.

![Evolution ultimate](/img/iris/image007.png)

## Evolution dim

An inline machine for measuring and inspecting bottle dimensions.
