As a global leader in glass container inspection, **IRIS Inspection Machines** redefines industry standards with its next-generation **EVOLUTION NEO** series and artificial intelligence (AI) technologies. Designed to minimize operator dependency, streamline job changes, and enable real-time connectivity between hot-end and cold-end operations, IRIS inspection systems deliver maximum precision for zero-defect production.

## WENSPECT – Complete Cold End Line Alliance

- **Synergy of IRIS & Heye International:** Combining IRIS's expertise in camera inspection with Heye International's mechanical and starwheel check inspection technology, WENSPECT offers turnkey, full-line quality control solutions for the cold end of container glass plants.
- **Full Data Connectivity:** All inspection equipment along the line communicates seamlessly with each other and plant management systems, providing centralized control and rich data analytics.

## Evolution NEO – Ultimate (All-in-One Multi-Function Inspection Machine)

- **Combined Sidewall, Base & Finish Inspection:** The flagship EVOLUTION NEO Ultimate integrates body/sidewall, base, finish, and verticality inspections into a single, compact machine footprint.
- **NEO Intelligence:** Powered by advanced AI algorithms, it automatically classifies complex defects. Operators simply define defect size thresholds while the machine identifies defect types (stones, blisters, checks, etc.) autonomously.
- **Space-Saving Efficiency:** Delivers comprehensive, single-station quality control while optimizing valuable floor space on high-speed production lines.

<img src="/img/iris/evolution-neo-ultimate.jpg" alt="Evolution NEO Ultimate" width="360" />

## Evolution NEO – Sidewall (Sidewall & Body Inspection Machine)

- **AI-Powered Defect Recognition:** Detects body defects such as stones, blisters, inclusions, and dimensional deformations using high-resolution cameras. With **NEO Intelligence**, defect classification is entirely automated.
- **3D Engraving & Mold Number Filtering:** Advanced 3D modeling isolates logos, embossing, and mold numbers to eliminate false rejects and highlight true defects.
- **Hot End Connected:** Shares defect images and data in real time with IS machine operators at the hot end, enabling immediate corrective actions to boost overall pack-to-melt ratios.

<img src="/img/iris/evolution-neo-sidewall.jpg" alt="Evolution NEO Sidewall" width="360" />

## Evolution NEO – Base & Finish (Base, Finish & Verticality Inspection Machine)

- **Comprehensive Base & Finish Inspection:** Performs high-precision inspection of base defects (cracks, stones, contamination) and finish geometries (inner/outer diameter, deformities, finish checks).
- **Non-Contact Verticality Measurement:** Accurately measures container verticality and lean without physical contact.
- **Fast & Repeatable Job Changes:** Features a comprehensive pre-set library, drastically reducing adjustment times during article changeovers.

<img src="/img/iris/evolution-neo-base-finish.jpg" alt="Evolution NEO Base & Finish" width="360" />

## Oculus NEO (Camera-Based Check Inspection System)

- **Advanced Check Detection:** Developed in partnership with Video Systems, OCULUS NEO utilizes compact high-resolution cameras and advanced optical algorithms to accurately detect checks—one of the most critical defect types in glass manufacturing.
- **Universal Integration:** Designed for universal compatibility, easily retrofitting into any carousel inspection machine on the market.
- **iBot AI Assistant Support:** Fully compatible with IRIS's iBot AI assistant, advancing smart factory (Industry 4.0) initiatives and significantly reducing scrap rates.
