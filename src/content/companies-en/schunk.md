**Schunk Ingenieurkeramik GmbH** is part of the **Schunk Group**, based in Giessen, Germany. Today it is one of the world's leading manufacturers of silicon carbide and aluminium oxide based ceramics.

## Sanitaryware

Silicon-infiltrated, reaction-bonded silicon carbide (RBSiC) materials, produced under the trade name **CarSIK-Z**, can be used safely at temperatures up to 1380°C. Compared with materials such as recrystallised silicon carbide (RSiC) and nitride-bonded silicon carbide (NSiC), the excellent properties of RBSiC materials make them the most suitable choice within a given temperature range. Typical applications for this product are support elements and beams on kiln cars. Thanks to their outstanding performance in service and high reliability in use, **CarSIK-Z** beams and supports hold a significant worldwide market share in sanitaryware ceramics and in heavy-load electrical porcelain applications.

Advantages:

- Excellent oxidation and corrosion resistance thanks to the absence of porosity
- Very good thermal shock resistance
- High modulus of rupture
- Excellent long-term mechanical strength
- Low bulk density
- High surface quality
- Clean internal surfaces

## Porcelain

Nitride-bonded silicon carbide (NSiC) materials, produced under the trade name **CarSIK-NG**, offer improved properties over recrystallised silicon carbide (RSiC) materials, such as higher strength and a higher application temperature. Batts ranging from 6 to 8 mm in thickness distribute heat homogeneously within the kiln and so save energy. **CarSIK-NG** batts are also used on kiln cars in sanitaryware ceramic production.

## Burner Tubes

Process changes have widened the range of applications for ceramic burners, and in many of them ceramic burners have replaced metal ones. Here too, silicon-infiltrated reaction-bonded silicon carbide (RBSiC) recuperator and radiant tubes have gained importance thanks to their excellent properties. Firing technology has changed considerably with the emergence of low-NOx firing concepts and modified RBSiC ceramics.

Through its research and development work and close cooperation with customers across different market segments, **SCHUNK** has also developed a material named **CarSIK-GG**, which allows operation at temperatures as high as 1500°C. Radiant tubes used in indirect heating transfer heat by radiation, and the combustion gases are not allowed to come into contact with the material being fired. **CarSIK-G** and **CarSIK-GG** tubes fully meet the high demands that radiant tube applications place on the material.

## Alumina Tubes

Alumina tubes are produced open at both ends or closed at one end, with or without flanges, in the length and diameter required for the customer's application.

Advantages:

- High dimensional tolerance
- Chemical wear resistance
- High thermal resistance
- Very good thermal shock resistance
- A high degree of reliability
- Economical
