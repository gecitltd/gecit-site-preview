As the official representative of **Rondot Group**—a global leader in high-performance equipment and solutions for the container glass manufacturing industry—we bring industry-leading technology and expertise to your production lines. We offer Rondot Group's specialized hot-end brands to enhance efficiency, optimize line performance, and meet the modernization requirements of glass manufacturing plants.

## Rondot – Delivery, Variable & Measuring Equipment

As the flagship brand of the group, Rondot specializes in glass scoop/delivery equipment, variable consumables, and high-precision hot-end measuring devices. It ensures that molten glass gobs are directed into the IS machine molds at optimal speed, angle, and shape, significantly improving product quality and overall line efficiency.

## Sheppee – Hot End Ware Handling Solutions

Sheppee sets global standards in the delicate transfer of hot glass containers from the IS machine to the annealing lehr. Featuring innovative pushers, cross transfer systems, and high-speed line conveyors, Sheppee guarantees the safe and seamless transportation of hot glass without deformation.

## Novaxion – Automated Swabbing Systems & Data Acquisition

Novaxion develops robotic swabbing systems and data acquisition technology designed to automate the mold swabbing process during glass forming. By replacing manual swabbing, it eliminates safety hazards and production drops, providing consistent mold lubrication, extending mold lifespan, and maintaining uniform product quality.

## Sonicam – Mold Maintenance & Cleaning Technology

Sonicam manufactures high-precision equipment specifically engineered for the maintenance, polishing, and cleaning of glass container molds. Its advanced ultrasonic cleaning baths and mold maintenance workshops preserve original mold specifications and minimize costly production downtime.

## Graphoidal – Hot End Lubrication & Spraying Systems

Graphoidal designs advanced precision spraying systems for hot-end lubrication in glass forming processes. Offering specialized solutions for shear blade lubrication, scoop spraying, and blank mold lubrication, it delivers maximum operational efficiency and cleanliness with minimal oil consumption.
