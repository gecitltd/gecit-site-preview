Brazilian-based **Estiva Refratarios** manufactures kiln refractory materials for the ceramics sector and provides solutions in this field, with more than 26 years of experience. It develops kiln and car designs and materials for the production of sanitaryware, porcelain and ceramic tiles. A leader in the production of cordierite, mullite and alumina-based kiln furniture and ceramic rollers, **Estiva Refratarios** combines world-class quality and reliability through its projects, technical innovations and products.

Products are developed using raw materials selected through rigorous quality control procedures. The entire production process uses latest-generation technology and is supervised by expert personnel. Projects are developed by the engineering team according to each company's needs, taking the technical innovations of the market into account.

## Products

- Kiln refractories
- Refractory blocks for kiln cars
- Columns and column caps
- Kitchen ceramics
- Extruded solid plates
- Extruded grooved plates
- Pressed shapes
- Ceramic rollers
- Special pieces for sanitaryware firing
- Rings for sanitaryware firing
- Supports for sanitaryware firing
