For 65 years, **PIETRO BONAITI S.r.l.** has been manufacturing metal belt conveyors for ovens used across a wide range of industries, including glass decorating and annealing lehrs, food processing plants and sintering kilns for ceramic materials. Drawing on years of experience and its technological and commercial know-how, the company meets its customers' application-specific requirements to a high standard. It helps them select the most suitable belt type and material for each specific application and use it correctly. It also replaces the parts required for wire belt maintenance and repairs broken or damaged sections.

The **Pietro Bonaiti S.r.l.** website shows the wide range of conveyor belt types it produces, whose structural designs have been developed over many years.
