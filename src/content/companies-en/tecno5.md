**Tecno 5 s.r.l.** manufactures machines and accessories that screen print designs in three to eight colours onto cylindrical, conical and other shaped items such as glass bottles, tumblers, ashtrays, jars and bowls. The company belongs to the Italian **Cerve Group**, founded in 1953. Given that the Cerve group prints around 1,500,000 pieces a day, it is clear that Tecno 5 machines are the product of extensive experience.

1. Simplicity
2. Quality
3. Innovation
4. Reliability
5. Service

These five key points reflect Tecno 5's philosophy, focused on customer satisfaction.

- **QUALITY** — High colour performance and printing precision.
- **RELIABILITY** — Its characteristic features make the machine a lifetime investment: a robust structure, readiness for continuous 24-hour production, and top-quality parts with a high safety factor.
- **SIMPLICITY** — This is what produces high efficiency and flexibility: high production performance, short product changeover and set-up times, low-cost decoration tooling, easy relocation thanks to four heavy-duty wheels, the ability to print a wide variety of shapes and sizes on the same machine, decoration accessories that can be used across different machine types, and operation that requires no specialist skill.
- **INNOVATION** — The product range is continuously renewed in line with customer needs and new market demands.
- **SERVICE** — Highly qualified engineers work on new ideas to develop the machines according to specific customer requirements. Part of the factory is dedicated to customer training, introducing Tecno 5 machines both theoretically and practically. A large spare parts stock is held so that customer needs can be met as quickly as possible. Worldwide technical assistance is guaranteed by an experienced and qualified team of technicians.
