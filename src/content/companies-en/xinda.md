**Xinda Refractory** (Lengshuijiang Xinda Refractories Manufacturing Co., Ltd.) is an established manufacturer specializing in R&D and production of silica refractories for float glass, container glass, and industrial furnaces. With an annual capacity of 80,000 tons, advanced molding equipment, and strict quality control standards, Xinda exports high-performance refractory materials to Germany, France, Italy, Brazil, and major global markets.
