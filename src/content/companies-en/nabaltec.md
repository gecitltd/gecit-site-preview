Founded in 1936 by VAW Aluminium AG as an aluminium oxide production centre, **NABALTEC** became an independent company after acquiring the Nabwerk plant in Schwandorf, Bavaria, Germany, in 1995. With a product range shaped around customer requirements, **NABALTEC** holds a strong worldwide market position in high-quality aluminium oxide products. The company places great importance on quality assurance and quality management and holds DIN EN ISO 9001 certification. Aluminium oxide (Al2O3) is the most important ceramic raw material used in the refractory industry, in polishing products and in high-technology applications. As a flame-retardant filler, aluminium oxide also allows plastics to be used safely.

## NABALOX®

Nabaltec produces calcined aluminium oxide for the refractory and ceramic industries using the BAYER process. In the selection of raw materials, attention is paid to key parameters such as chemical purity, particle size distribution, crystal size and crystal structure. The calcination process determines the alpha Al2O3 content and the structure, size and morphology of the primary crystals. The desired product properties are optimised through grinding, screening, granulation and forming.

**Nabalox®** aluminas offer properties such as high wear and impact resistance, electrical insulation, high mechanical strength, high temperature resistance, dimensional accuracy and optimised chemical durability.

## SYMULOX®

The synthetic sintered mullites Nabaltec produces under the trade names M72 and M85 are used as high-quality raw materials in a wide range of refractory applications. Their homogeneous phase structure and very low impurity content are advantages over natural aluminosilicates. Synthetic mullites offer good chemical integrity, high refractory qualities and excellent high-temperature durability.

## ACTILOX®

Obtained by flash calcination of specially selected hydroxides, **Actilox® T40** has very high chemical reactivity. Typical applications: low-alkali accelerator for refractory concrete; binder for low-cement and cement-free shaped and unshaped refractory masses; raw material for catalysts and catalyst carriers.

Pseudoboehmite-based **Actilox® P40** and **S40** have a very high specific surface area and a defined pore volume. Typical applications: raw material for catalysts and catalyst carriers; raw material for sol-gel applications.

## GRANALOX®

Nabaltec produces ready-to-press ceramic bodies for isostatic parts, containing 92%, 96% and 99% Al2O3. These ceramic bodies suit applications requiring the properties characteristic of corundum materials: high hardness, high wear resistance, good thermal shock resistance, electrical insulation at high temperatures, thermal conductivity and very good corrosion resistance.

The product range also includes **APYMAG®**, a magnesium oxide-based, high-temperature-resistant flame-retardant filler, and **APYRAL®**, an aluminium oxide-based, environmentally friendly and non-toxic flame-retardant filler.
