**Falorni Tech** specialises in glass melting technologies, covering regenerative, recuperative, oxy-fuel and oxy-fuel/electric furnace types. Beyond integrated Engineering–Procurement–Construction projects, the company also serves as a trusted advisor to corporate and private investors in the glass industry, for both container and flat glass.
