**Vidremot** is a leading specialist in the manufacturing of high-quality plungers, coolers, and critical IS machine spare parts for the container glass industry. Utilizing advanced HVOF (High Velocity Oxygen Fuel) thermal spraying technology across its entire range of NNPB plungers, wide-mouth plungers, and blow plugs, Vidremot delivers superior wear resistance, extended operational lifespan, and outstanding forming precision.
